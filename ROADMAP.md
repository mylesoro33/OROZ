# Roadmap

- **iPhone/Android app (Capacitor):** Apple Reminders, device contacts, and background notifications for bedtime and due dates.
- **Cloud sync (Supabase):** use the same `Repository` interface, opt-in only.
- **Schoology and Canvas APIs:** richer assignment details through per-school developer keys.
- **Friends' availability:** opt-in free/busy sharing to plan birthdays together.
- **Smarter evenings:** suggest study blocks that finish before wind-down, based on what's due.
