# Roadmap

- **iPhone/Android app (Capacitor):** Apple Reminders, device contacts and calendars, and background notifications.
- **Microsoft sign-in (Graph):** read Outlook and Teams directly instead of through a published link.
- **Cloud sync:** using the same `Repository` interface, opt-in.
- **Location:** quiet route-aware errand suggestions from your own places.
- **Learning:** adjust meal and sleep bands from what actually happens, with every change shown in Profile.
